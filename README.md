## How the work runs

Every build follows one pattern, without exception:

> **capture → AI extracts structured signal → a named human approves the diff → system of
> record → auto reporting**

Two rules hold everywhere:

1. **Capture is the bottleneck, not the tool.** Most "AI builds" are really data-access projects
   wearing a costume. Where that's true here, the build says so.
2. **Nothing becomes a record of truth without a named human approving it.** No owner, no build.

That second rule comes from AiDA — VGG's earlier HR assistant, which stopped working when the
engineer who built it left. Every build here is designed so that can't happen again.

## What would move this fastest

Four things, in order. Each unblocks more than itself.

1. **Fireflies access + the data-handling policy** (B2). Nine builds sit behind it, including the
   FBI Files and the Context Brief engine. This is the single biggest unlock on the board.
2. **A decision on the six unplaced builds** — B5, B24, B37, B39, B45, B47. They're in the backlog
   and absent from the capability architecture. Proposed homes are in
   [CAPABILITIES.md](CAPABILITIES.md); they need confirming.
3. **A second builder, or a longer timeline.** Sequential delivery of the nine capabilities is
   26–31 weeks. Overlapping phases II and III gets it to 18–22, and that requires funding a second
   person. One or the other has to be chosen; the plan doesn't work without it.
4. **The canonical pipeline decision** — OpenOps `deal_pipeline` or the Attio CRM. Two systems are
   being built toward and only one can be the record.

## A note on numbering

The B-numbers are a working backlog, not a product catalogue. They were assigned as builds were
discovered, so they don't run in priority order and three of them collide with the MindLab set.
[ID-MAP.md](ID-MAP.md) resolves every alias and duplicate. When in doubt, name the build, not
the number.
